================================================================================
REWARD DESIGN PATTERNS DISCOVERED
================================================================================

1. DEATH PENALTIES ARE CRITICAL
   - Range from -50 (Q-learning) to -1000 (Maverick)
   - Must prevent "death spirals" where agent gives up
   - Maverick uses 10x more aggressive penalty

2. DENSITY VS SPARSITY TRADEOFF
   - Q-Learning: Very dense (directional feedback every step)
   - PPO: Moderate (game events + shaped bonuses)
   - DQN Final: Sparse base (only real events), dense shaping
   - Maverick: Direct (only real events)

3. SAFETY SOPHISTICATION INCREASES WITH AGENT COMPLEXITY
   - Q-Learning: Simple danger levels (none/low/high/critical)
   - PPO: Pre-computed danger maps for all positions
   - DQN Final: Time-to-blast grids, escape route BFS, shelter detection
   - Maverick: Heavy penalties only

4. NORMALIZATION MATTERS
   - PPO explicitly scales to [-1, 1] for numerical stability
   - Others use raw scales (varying widely)
   - Normalization improves training convergence

5. EXPLORATION STRATEGIES DIFFER
   - Q-Learning: Pure epsilon-greedy
   - PPO: Entropy regularization + explicit exploration bonus
   - DQN Final: Epsilon-greedy with policy shaping
   - Maverick: Epsilon-greedy + rule-based fallback

6. BOMB STRATEGY ALIGNMENT
   - Q-Learning: Discourages (-0.5, no crates in training)
   - PPO: Safe bombing reward (0.4 for damage without death)
   - DQN Final: Heavy policy shaping (20+ parameters)
   - Maverick: Immediate feedback (crate_count * 33)

7. CUSTOM EVENT GENERATION
   - Q-Learning: 6 custom events (direction, danger, safety)
   - PPO: 5+ shaped rewards (computed dynamically)
   - DQN Final: 4 shaping components (safety, blast, shelter, TTB)
   - Maverick: 1 custom (immediate crate prediction)
